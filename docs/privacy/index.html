Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026 · Version 1.0.0

1. The short version

Neon Flip is a one-touch arcade game. We designed it so it collects as little as possible:

  • No accountNo nameNo emailNo locationNo contacts
  • Your scores & settings stay on your device
  • Ads are served by Google AdMob — it uses your device's advertising ID (see §3)

The full details follow, in plain language.

2. About Neon Flip

Neon Flip ("the game", "we") is a free arcade runner for Android, published by Michael Unkai. You tap to flip gravity, dodge neon obstacles and chase high scores. The game is free, supported by advertising, and offers optional purchases (remove ads, VIP badge).

4. Analytics (local only)

Any analytics or crash information in Neon Flip v1.0.0 is local-only: it stays on your device and is never uploaded anywhere. We plan to keep it that way; if this ever changes, we will update this policy and the Play Store Data Safety form before it does.

6. Ads — Google AdMob

Neon Flip shows banner, interstitial, and optional rewarded video ads through Google AdMob (Google Ireland/Google LLC). Google's own privacy policy explains how it uses this data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

  • Rewarded ads always require your tap — they are never forced.
  • You can opt out of ad personalization in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads (delete/reset advertising ID).
  • Manage Google ad settings at adssettings.google.com.

7. Purchases

Optional purchases (one-time "remove ads", optional VIP subscription) are processed exclusively by Google Play Billing. We never receive or store payment details. Payments, refunds, and subscriptions are governed by Google Play's terms. You can cancel the VIP subscription anytime via Google Play → Profile → Payments & subscriptions.

8. Children's privacy

Neon Flip is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone — including children — because the app has no accounts, no registration, and no analytics upload. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it promptly.

9. Data retention & deletion

  • On-device data (scores, settings, progress): delete by uninstalling the app or via Android Settings → Apps → Neon Flip → Storage → Clear data. Nothing remains on our side, because we never received it.
  • Ad data: controlled by Google — reset your advertising ID in Android settings or use your Google Account privacy controls.
  • Deletion requests: email michaelunkai@gmail.com and we will respond within 30 days.

10. Security & your rights

Network communication (ads, billing) is encrypted in transit (TLS). Because we hold no personal databases, the main practical protection is that your data simply stays with you. Where applicable law grants rights (access, correction, deletion, objection), you can exercise them by contacting us — or, for most data, by using your device and Google account controls, since that is where the data lives.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change how Neon Flip handles data, we will update this page and change the "last updated" date, and update the Google Play Data Safety form accordingly.
