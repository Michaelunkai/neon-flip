## Full description (≤4000 chars — current: 2138)

```
Neon Flip is a one-touch arcade runner where gravity is your only control. Tap to flip the world upside down, glide along neon rails, and squeeze through impossible gaps — every run is a pulse-pounding chase for one more meter, one more coin, one more personal best.

HOW IT FEELS
You never steer. You never stop. One tap flips gravity and the entire world tilts with you. Straights become ceilings, ceilings become floors, and the only thing between you and a wall of neon is your timing.

WHY YOU'LL KEEP COMING BACK
• One-touch controls — playable anywhere, anytime, in seconds
• Endless, procedurally generated tracks — no two runs are alike
• Thrilling gravity-flip physics tuned to feel smooth and fair
• Glowing retro-neon art style with reactive sound
• Coin pickups, unlockable ball skins, and a lifetime best-score record
• Plays offline — no Wi-Fi needed for the core game
• Tiny download, smooth performance even on older phones
• New high scores, new near-misses, new bragging rights — every single run

FREE, HONEST, AND TRANSPARENT
Neon Flip is 100% free to play and fully playable without paying a cent. The game is supported by ads, and shows:
• Banner ads (during menus)
• Interstitial ads (between runs)
• Rewarded video ads — always optional: watch one for bonus coins or a second chance

Want zero ads forever? One optional in-app purchase removes all ads permanently. A clearly labeled optional VIP subscription exists for supporters who want to back the game's development — it unlocks a golden trail and a VIP badge. Nothing is locked behind a paywall: every track, every skin path, and the whole game are free.

YOUR PRIVACY
The game works offline and your scores, settings, and progress stay on your device. We never ask for your name, email, or location. See the privacy policy for the few details that matter, in plain language.

TIPS FOR NEW RUNNERS
• Tap rhythm, not speed — smooth flips beat frantic ones
• Grab coin lines: they mark the safe path more often than not
• Rewarded second chances are best saved for a personal-best run

Download Neon Flip now — one tap stands between you and a new high score.
```
